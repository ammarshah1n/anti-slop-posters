# Directions

Pick **one**. Do not blend two. A direction is a complete set of decisions, so
the poster looks like one person made it on purpose.

State your choice in one line before building:
`Direction: Riso gig. Hero: the band name, one word per line. Accent: the date.`

| # | Name | Use for | Palette (`color.md`) | Fonts (`type.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Riso gig | gigs, club nights, zines | Newsprint | Big Shoulders Display + DM Mono |
| 2 | Swiss | exhibitions, conferences, formal events | Chalk | Archivo (width axis) |
| 3 | Editorial | talks, readings, launches, essays | Bone and ink | Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans |
| 4 | Flat colour | markets, community, sales, fundraisers | Market | Bricolage Grotesque |
| 5 | Night | film, music, after-dark events | Night | Clash Display + IBM Plex Mono |
| 6 | Heritage | food, craft, makers, farm, cafe | Moss | Fraunces + Work Sans |
| 7 | Brutalist | clubs, fashion drops, anything loud and raw | Concrete | Archivo Black + JetBrains Mono |
| 8 | Photo-led | one great photograph is the idea | pick from the photo | any pairing above |

Each direction pairs with a composition recipe (A-E in `layout.md`): Riso, Flat, Brutalist
use A or E; Swiss uses B; Editorial and Heritage use C; Photo-led uses D; Night uses E.

Examples 1-4 are built in `template/examples/` (`riso.html`, `swiss.html`,
`editorial.html`, `flat.html`). Open the nearest one and adapt it.

---

## 1. Riso gig
- **Layout:** Title huge, flush-left, one word per line, filling the width. Info block bottom-left in mono.
- **Finish:** Paper grain. Accent as a flat block or a single overprinted word. A deliberate 3-4px misregistration offset on one layer is fine.
- **Image:** None, or a high-contrast cut-out in ink with halftone dots.
- **Never:** gradients, neon, a band photo in the middle.

## 2. Swiss
- **Layout:** 6-column grid, strict. Hero is a single word, a numeral, or a date, in the left 4 columns. Small info text sits in the right 2 columns, aligned to a shared baseline. One thin rule.
- **Finish:** Flat, no texture.
- **Image:** Optional, rectangular, aligned to column edges.
- **Never:** rotated text, drop shadows, rounded anything.

## 3. Editorial
- **Layout:** Giant serif title with one italic phrase. Large clear margins. Info in a quiet sans column. A thin rule above the info.
- **Finish:** Warm paper, subtle grain.
- **Image:** A single small, well-cropped photograph or none.
- **Never:** a second serif, all-caps titles, gradient text.

## 4. Flat colour
- **Layout:** A saturated full-bleed colour field, one very heavy title, one shape (circle, arch, bar) cropped by the edge. Info in a solid block at the bottom.
- **Finish:** Flat plus faint grain.
- **Image:** A geometric shape or hand-drawn mark, not a photo.
- **Never:** clip-art, emoji, drop shadows.

## 5. Night
- **Layout:** Dark paper, warm ink, one small bright accent. Title mid-weight, not shouting. A lot of space. Info in mono along the bottom edge.
- **Finish:** Grain. **No glow**, no light leaks, no lens flare.
- **Image:** A single dark, grainy photograph with the black point lifted.
- **Never:** neon, purple-blue, particles, "cyber" anything.

## 6. Heritage
- **Layout:** A soft serif title, left, large. A small hand-made-feeling mark (stamp, label shape) as the accent. Info in a tidy two-column list.
- **Finish:** Warm paper with fibre grain.
- **Image:** A real photo of the food or product, close, natural light.
- **Never:** faux-vintage distress overlays, banner ribbons, script fonts.

## 7. Brutalist
- **Layout:** Everything big, tight and heavy. Hard edges. Text blocks butt against each other. Mono info set in full width rows.
- **Finish:** Flat, high contrast. One raw hairline grid is allowed.
- **Image:** Harsh, flash-lit, cropped.
- **Never:** soft anything.

## 8. Photo-led
- **Layout:** The photograph bleeds off at least two edges. Text sits on a flat area inside the image or on a solid strip. The type is small and precise, so the image carries it.
- **Finish:** Treat the photo into the palette (duotone, grain). Do not leave raw.
- **Never:** text over a busy area without a flat panel.

---

## Why one direction

Models blend. Asked for "modern, bold, elegant, playful", they produce the average
of all four, which is exactly what slop is. A single direction forces the
specific.
