# Redesign a poster you already have

For when you (or an AI) already made one and it looks off, and you cannot say why.
Use it with the master prompt loaded.

---

Here is an existing poster [ATTACH IMAGE or HTML]. Do not start designing yet.

1. **Diagnose.** List every slop tell you can see, using the master prompt's rules.
   For each: where it is, and why it reads as machine-made. Be blunt. Then list what
   is genuinely good and must be kept.
2. **Extract the facts.** Write out every piece of real information on it (names,
   dates, times, venue, price, URL) exactly as printed. Flag anything that looks
   invented or inconsistent and ask me to confirm it. Drop anything decorative.
3. **Decide.** Write the six-line decision block (Direction, Hero, Palette, Fonts,
   Format, Accent goes on). Choose a direction that fits the content, not the old look.
4. **Rebuild** from the template. Keep the facts, throw away the design. Do not
   carry over any of the original's layout, colours or fonts unless the diagnosis said
   they work.
5. **Show the delta.** In three lines: what you removed, what you changed, what you
   kept.

Never "tidy up" the old design. Starting over is faster and always better than
nudging slop.
